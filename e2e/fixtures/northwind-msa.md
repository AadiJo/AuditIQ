# MASTER SUBSCRIPTION, EQUIPMENT, AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement Date: June 15, 2026. Effective Date: July 1, 2026.
This Agreement is entered into between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Vendor") and Contoso Retail Group, LLC ("Customer"). Vendor provides in-store analytics software, sensor equipment, and related services. Customer operates retail stores and wishes to use Vendor's platform across its store network.

## Article 1. Definitions
1.1 Platform. "Platform" means Vendor's hosted analytics software known as Insight Cloud, including the modules listed in Exhibit A.
1.2 Devices. "Devices" means the Beacon S2 in-store sensors described in Exhibit A.
1.3 Store. "Store" means a retail location operated by Customer in which at least one Device is installed.
1.4 Go-Live. "Go-Live" means the date on which Customer signs a Go-Live Certificate confirming that the Platform processes live data from at least 25 Stores.

## Article 2. Subscription
2.1 Access. Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Platform during the Subscription Term for Customer's internal business purposes.
2.2 Subscription Term. The Subscription Term begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months.
2.3 Hosting. Vendor hosts the Platform using third-party cloud infrastructure selected by Vendor. Vendor remains fully responsible for availability, security, and the acts of its hosting providers.
2.4 Data Events. The subscription includes up to 50,000,000 data events per calendar month. Data events above that allocation are billed as Usage Fees under Section 6.3.

## Article 3. Devices
3.1 Purchase. Customer purchases forty (40) Devices at the prices in Exhibit A.
3.2 Delivery and Title. Vendor ships Devices FOB Vendor's warehouse. Title and risk of loss pass to Customer when Devices are delivered to the carrier.
3.3 Standard Warranty. Vendor warrants that each Device will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after delivery.
3.4 Extended Care. In addition to the standard warranty, Vendor will provide Extended Care for each Device for a further twenty-four (24) months, including advance replacement within two business days and quarterly firmware tuning visits. Extended Care is included in the Device price and is not sold separately under this Agreement.

## Article 4. Implementation Services
4.1 Scope. Vendor will configure the Platform, install Devices in up to 40 Stores, integrate the Platform with Customer's point-of-sale system, and train Customer staff (the "Implementation Services").
4.2 Implementation Fee. The Implementation Services are provided for a fixed fee of $36,000, invoiced fifty percent (50%) on the Agreement Date and fifty percent (50%) at Go-Live.
4.3 Acceptance. Customer will test the Implementation Services and sign a Go-Live Certificate or deliver a written list of material defects within ten (10) business days after Vendor gives notice that the Platform is ready. If Customer does neither, Go-Live is deemed to have occurred.
4.4 Late Go-Live Credit. If Go-Live has not occurred by October 31, 2026 for reasons within Vendor's control, Vendor will credit Customer $1,000 for each full week of delay, up to a maximum credit of $8,000.
4.5 Custom Reports. Upon Customer's written request, Vendor will develop custom reports at $180 per hour, not to exceed 200 hours without a signed change order.

## Article 5. Service Levels
5.1 Availability. Vendor will make the Platform available 99.5% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.
5.2 Service Credits. If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, Customer is entitled to a credit of 5% of that month's Subscription Fee for each full 0.5% below the commitment, up to 25% of that month's Subscription Fee. Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for unavailability.

## Article 6. Fees and Payment
6.1 Subscription Fee. The Subscription Fee is $8,000 per month for up to 300 Stores, invoiced quarterly in advance.
6.2 Volume Pricing. If Customer operates more than 300 Stores at any time during the Subscription Term, the per-Store fee for all Stores falls by 10% for the entire Subscription Term, and Vendor will credit Customer for the difference on fees already paid.
6.3 Usage Fees. Data events above the monthly allocation in Section 2.4 are billed monthly in arrears at $0.02 per thousand events.
6.4 Marketing Development Credit. Vendor will pay Customer a one-time marketing development credit of $5,000, applied against Customer's first invoice, to support Customer's in-store announcement of the Platform. Customer is not required to provide any distinct good or service in exchange.
6.5 Payment Terms. Invoices are due sixty (60) days after the invoice date. Late amounts accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month.
6.6 Most Favored Customer. If during the Subscription Term Vendor offers the Platform to a similarly sized retail customer at a lower per-Store price, Vendor will reduce Customer's Subscription Fee to match for the remainder of the Subscription Term.

## Article 7. Renewal
7.1 Renewal Option. Customer may renew the subscription for one additional twelve (12) month term by giving written notice at least sixty (60) days before the Subscription Term ends.
7.2 Renewal Price. The Subscription Fee for the renewal term will be Vendor's then-current list price less twenty percent (20%). Vendor does not offer this discount to customers who have not purchased an initial twenty-four month subscription.

## Article 8. Term and Termination
8.1 Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and ends when the Subscription Term, including any renewal term, ends.
8.2 Termination for Convenience. After the first twelve (12) months of the Subscription Term, Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice. Vendor will refund any prepaid Subscription Fees for the period after termination. Implementation Fees and Device purchases are not refundable.
8.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

## Article 9. General
9.1 Confidentiality. Each party will protect the other party's confidential information with at least reasonable care and use it only to perform this Agreement.
9.2 Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality, neither party's liability will exceed the fees paid under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the claim.
9.3 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.
9.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, including its Exhibits, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.
